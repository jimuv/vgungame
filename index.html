How to Play

Welcome to the game! Use the WASD keys to move the ball. Click the "Menu" button to open the menu and control the ball.

There will be a shop where you can buy new weapons and items. Everything is for free except for the Crown, which you can obtain by getting $10 from 1 enemy

Press 'F' to shoot a yellow ball from the center of the screen.
